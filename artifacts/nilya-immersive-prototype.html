Real-data boundary

Quantity, stock, variants, ratings, discounts, favorite counts, promotional labels, view counts, demand, seller badges, and delivery estimates remain absent unless supported by real schema fields and populated records. Every listing read and write remains NEW-only. The visual prototype stores presentation state locally and performs no application or Supabase writes.
